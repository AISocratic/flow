12Self-feeding sweeps

The system finds its own bugs while everyone sleeps.

Nightly, a visual-QA sweep screenshots every route on desktop and mobile and has a vision model judge them; a performance sweep scores the same routes on speed and web vitals. Findings become bug cards — deduplicated by title, so a known issue never files twice — dropped straight into Todo at a priority matching their severity. By morning, the board has already triaged the night's regressions.
