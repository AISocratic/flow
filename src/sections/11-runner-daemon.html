11Runner daemon

The always-on autopilot.

A long-running process that makes the loop continuous instead of on-demand. It scans the board, claims ready cards race-safely (two daemons never grab the same card), executes each through the standard worktree pipeline, merges or parks the result per the card's autonomy policy, and alerts a human — Telegram, SMS — only when something genuinely needs one. With the daemon on, filing a card is shipping the feature.
