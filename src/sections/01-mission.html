01Mission

The highest-level object. Only a human may change it.

The mission defines what the company exists to accomplish, and it is the long-term constraint under which every goal, project and agent operates. Unlike everything downstream, it can only be created or changed by the human operator. Agents may read it, plan against it, ask clarifying questions and flag conflicts — but they cannot rewrite it, and no metric, anomaly or evaluation is allowed to.

A mission should stay stable for roughly a year. It is not measured directly: humans translate it into the quarterly goals and KPIs the system can actually act on, and every goal, project, epic and task must be traceable back to it.

Missions look like this
  • Ensure everyone can own and benefit from AI.
  • Make professional software development accessible to any small team.
  • Build the most trusted technical community for frontier AI.

This replaces the assumption that a planning agent creates a new project every week. A weekly ritual may still create or prioritise work, but a project is a persistent strategic container holding successive epics — not a disposable weekly plan.
