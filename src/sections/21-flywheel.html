The flywheel

Each pass around the loop compounds. Shipped work moves the metrics; metrics expose the next gap; monitoring turns the gap into cards; agents turn cards into shipped work — and the agents' memories mean every pass is a little sharper than the last. The system's output is not just features: it is an ever-better version of itself.

Humans set direction

Define goals, adjust targets, decide what the company is for. Everything downstream is derived.

Humans gate what they choose

Per-card autonomy means review effort concentrates exactly where judgment matters — and nowhere else.

Humans can just watch

Manager UI and dashboards make the whole machine legible in real time. Observing is a valid mode of operation.
